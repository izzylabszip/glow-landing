Most weddings in Nepal happen in a few auspicious windows: Mangsir, then Magh and Falgun, and again in Baisakh and Jestha. That means the best makeup artists in Kathmandu are booked by many brides for the same dates. Planning early is the difference between choosing your artist and taking whoever is free.

## Two to three months before

- **Choose your look.** Newari bridal with haku patasi, a red and gold lehenga, a soft modern bridal look, or a different look for each event.
- **Shortlist artists from their real work.** Look for brides with a similar skin tone and outfit to yours.
- **Book your date.** For Mangsir weddings, aim for 6 to 8 weeks ahead at the latest.

## Four to six weeks before: the trial

A trial look is worth it. Wear something close to your wedding colours, and take photos in daylight and with flash.

Tell your artist:

1. Every event and its timing: mehendi, wedding, reception
2. Your outfit, jewellery and how the saree or lehenga will be draped
3. Anything your skin reacts to
4. Whether they will come to your home and how early

## Skin prep, starting now

Winter wedding season means dry air. Steady care works better than trying something new the week before.

- Keep a simple routine: gentle cleanser, moisturiser, and sunscreen in the day
- Do not start strong new actives or facials in the last two weeks
- Drink water and sleep. It shows

A weekly skin scan in Glow can show whether dryness, redness or texture is improving as the date gets closer. It is a guide for your routine, not a medical opinion, so see a dermatologist about anything that worries you.

## The week of the wedding

- Confirm timings with your artist, hair stylist and mehendi artist
- Book mehendi one or two days before so the colour deepens
- Shape brows and do any threading a few days ahead, not the morning of

## On the day

Leave more time than you think. Bridal makeup with hair and draping usually takes two to three hours. Keep a small touch-up kit with the lip colour, blotting papers and a little powder.
