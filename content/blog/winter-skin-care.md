When the cold months arrive, mornings get colder and the air gets drier. Many people notice tight, flaky or red skin, and makeup that looks patchy by midday. A few changes to your routine help a lot.

## Why winter is hard on skin

- Cold, dry air pulls moisture out of the top layer of skin
- Hot showers and heaters dry it further
- Winter sun still reaches your skin, so sunscreen still matters

## A simple winter routine

**Morning**

1. A gentle, non-foaming cleanser, or just water if your skin is very dry
2. A moisturiser a little richer than your summer one
3. Sunscreen, every day

**Evening**

1. Cleanse off sunscreen and makeup gently
2. Moisturiser, a little thicker at night
3. A lip balm before bed

Keep it simple. Strong scrubs and new active products are more likely to irritate skin when it is already dry.

## Makeup that does not cling to dry patches

- Hydrate first and let your moisturiser sink in for a few minutes
- Use cream blush and cream or liquid base instead of heavy powder
- Set only where you get shiny, usually the nose
- Carry a hydrating mist rather than more powder
